# Legacy Source 1 CS:GO

## Objective

Implement legacy Source 1 Counter-Strike: Global Offensive behavior after the TF2 and CS:S foundations. Source 2 is out of scope.

## Responsibilities

- Own legacy CS:GO players, weapons, economy, teams, movement differences, entities, objectives, rules, replicated state, and presentation mappings.
- Provide the game behavior used by every legacy CS:GO ruleset and product.

## Non-Responsibilities

- Source 2 formats, interfaces, terminology, or compatibility.
- Generic Source behavior or CS:S differences.
- Mode behavior owned by a legacy CS:GO ruleset.

## Relationships

Will compose generic packages and supply game behavior to [`rulesets/`](rulesets/) and legacy CS:GO applications.

## Completion

Complete when the future declared legacy Source 1 CS:GO behavior universe is handled with credible evidence.
