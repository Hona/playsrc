## Composition Contract

A composition contains exactly one `Primary` item, zero or more `Modifier` items in stable inventory order, and zero or one `Policy` item. The three policy items are mutually exclusive. A modifier never owns the primary win condition. Training, item testing, and jump are complete primary selections and do not compose with a stock primary.

The common TF2 lifecycle is `waiting-for-players → pre-round/setup → active → team-win or stalemate → bonus/between-round → reset`; `games/tf2` owns those states and generic transitions. Each primary row below owns the exact mode-specific guards, objective transitions, timer effects, winning-team request, overtime predicate, stalemate disposition, spawn selection, and reset additions applied within that lifecycle.

Every item currently has coverage classification `Missing`: no accepted child roadmap and implementation supply its complete contract. `Registered` and `Proposed` below describe root owner registration, not delivery status.
