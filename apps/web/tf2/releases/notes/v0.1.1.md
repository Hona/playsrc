# playsrc v0.1.1

This patch fixes startup with older saved loadouts, keeps equipment navigation usable while models load, and restores authored team-selection door transitions.

The 15-map selection and 64-item local inventory are unchanged from v0.1.0.

## Loading and Interface

- **Saved loadouts:** Profiles saved before Demoman's stock launchers became available now upgrade without the `TransitionFailed` startup screen. Existing equipment and unrelated settings are preserved; no cache or saved profile is cleared. [#275](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/275)
- **Equipment navigation:** Back and Escape remain usable during model preparation. Closing or replacing a page cancels uncommitted equip requests instead of replaying stale navigation. Keyboard focus, held-button handling and failure reporting retain their correct owner. Controls share identical rasterized artwork without changing alpha or clipping. [#268](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/268)
- **Team-selection doors:** Hover animations retain their authored direction, cross-fade through interrupted transitions, settle at the final pose and reset correctly when reopened. Disabled-door hover and visible team-state changes follow the authored timing. [#269](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/269)

## Audio and Rendering

- MP3 sample quantization uses standard 128-bit WebAssembly SIMD in the audio module, preserving exact decoded samples. Gameplay compilation and simulation cadence are unchanged. [#270](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/270)
- Particle decoding avoids rebuilding duplicate system-identity strings for consecutive particles from the same effect, without retaining packet bytes or changing rendered output. [#274](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/274)

These focused allocation and decoder changes do not establish an end-to-end frame-rate improvement or resolve the reported long-frame freezes. Play remains local, with bots; this patch adds no maps or weapons.
