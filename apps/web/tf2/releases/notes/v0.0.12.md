# playsrc v0.0.12

Ten more Control Point and King of the Hill maps, ten rocket and flare unlocks, and environmental soundscapes join local TF2. This update also fixes the setup-countdown audio crash, damage-indicator rotation, heavy-combat frame rejection, and a Minigun ownership failure during class changes.

The release expands the published map set from five to fifteen. It remains an incremental local-play and bot release: performance is not uniformly better in every recorded comparison, stable 60+ Windows gameplay across all workloads is not certified, and complete Payload cart and Demoman rigid-body gameplay remain unfinished.

## Rendering and Visual Effects

### Ropes, Sprites, Glows, and Sky Effects

The added maps render their configured rope chains, sprite modes, light glows, sun, and static spotlights. Rope state follows authored length, slack, node count, normal-map material, lighting, subdivision, wind/resting behavior, and accepted client-frame lifetimes. Rope removal and round reset preserve Source behavior; map replacement recreates the appropriate owners.

Thin cable mip levels no longer create invalid compressed GPU textures that can poison the world render and leave only the sky visible. Exact decoded sub-block mip alternatives preserve the selected texture level rather than padding it or lowering quality.

Opaque static props now write depth in their opaque state instead of remaining permanently translucent merely because they support distance fading. This fixes steam showing through solid console surfaces while retaining visible smoke around unobstructed edges and proper faded-state rendering.

Related pull request: [#207](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/207).

### Damage Indicator Orientation

The radial damage indicator rotates its authored quad with the correct sign. Verification covers real gameplay damage from eight horizontal bearings, camera turns, extreme pitch, vertical rocket damage, fractional-DPI resizing, and lifecycle cleanup. This is an orientation correction, not an invented replacement graphic or a blanket offset applied without directional tests.

Related pull request: [#241](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/241).

### Heavy-Combat Frame Admission

Particle records are no longer incorrectly charged against the separate 4,096 scene-object budget. Entity and particle bounds remain independent, and scalar validation remains enforced. A retained 4,171-item failure became a successful native combat check reaching 4,301 items, with class cancellation, replacement, and disconnect coverage.

This fixes an intermittent `render frame is invalid` failure seen around setup expiry and combat without dropping particles or weakening their own limits.

Related pull request: [#255](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/255).

### Water Targets and Particle Images

Water reflection/refraction targets initialize their complete attachments before pipeline compilation can sample the color backing. The change removes an unused initial allocation while preserving mutable target ownership, formats, resize behavior, shaders, and depth testing. The real headed target/consumer check covers two generations and original/resized dimensions, with matching pixel hashes and explicit attachment teardown.

Particle materials can share fully identical initialized single-frame compressed images within their existing owner. Material, UV/sheet, blend, depth, and generation state remain independent. Current-format tests and a headed diagnostic verify all 14 affected authored materials, independent UV/blend phases, opaque occlusion, draw order, and exact color/normal/depth output.

These changes passed correctness gates, but their recorded timing comparisons were mixed or unfavorable. No speedup, proven nonregression, physical GPU-residency reduction, or overall RAM reduction is claimed for either change.

Related pull requests: [#248](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/248) and [#254](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/254).

## Performance and Responsiveness

### Payload Retry Storms and One Simulation Clock

Failed Payload Push/Guard path searches now respect their Source repath timers instead of repeating full searches every simulation tick. Explicit movement, action, and teleport invalidation remain intact.

The browser no longer adds an independently phased 15 ms gate on top of Rust's fixed-step simulation. Browser frames observe the simulation; they do not create extra ticks. The deterministic scheduling regression covers fourteen phase offsets and preserves one publication per authoritative tick.

Recorded Windows Upward checks with 15 bots measured 66.970 completed submissions/s at 1280x720/DPR1 and 66.663 at 1689x1277/DPR1. The native-size run retained 66-67 submissions in every full second and 16-17 in every full 250 ms bucket, with 400 simulation ticks in six seconds. These results belong to their recorded implementation build, not a blanket certification of every later integration or map. Native compositor events are kept separate from completed gameplay submissions.

A newer ordinary 23-bot 2Fort run at 1689x1277/DPR1 measured 52.462 completed submissions/s, with full-second counts of 45, 57, 59, 54, 49, and 50. Its frame gaps were 18.360/26.505/30.655/38.535 ms at p50/p95/p99/maximum. Stable 60+ remains unmet for that workload despite adequate available host memory.

Related pull request: [#237](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/237).

### Less Repeated Model and Compiler Work

Model lighting resolves one complete per-draw update owner rather than dispatching 43 or 44 separate member updates. Every used uniform still goes through its normal comparison and upload; no unchanged-value or same-object shortcut bypasses dependencies. Tests preserve shaders, bindings, mutable values, alternate actors, camera/pass changes, and teardown.

In matched six-second Mac captures, the inclusive node-owner CPU sampling estimate fell from 256.412 to 78.236 ms. A separate live correctness check compared 22,352 lighting values across 508 draws and six byte-identical color/normal/depth planes. CPU sampling estimates are not exclusive CPU wall time or a Windows FPS claim.

Exact model compiler-state reuse also reduces repeated preparation across compatible class/model draws while retaining primitive-owned textures and values. Controlled native cold model preparation fell from 4,534 to 2,763 ms. The original unfavorable loading comparison remains retained; controlled loading and memory checks passed. Residual cold team-to-class stalls remain and are not described as resolved by this one improvement.

Related pull requests: [#238](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/238) and [#249](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/249).

The remaining world-preparation owner now permits two independent native pipeline jobs while retaining serial initial-map scheduling and complete admission before Ready. Scene-owned particle graphs reuse exact compiler state: the current additive corpus uses 19 states instead of 43 while retaining all 43 preparation draws and matching WGSL, layouts, and values. Existing native proof is reused only for its audited unchanged scope; current-input compiler checks remain distinct from native pixel evidence. Unfavorable heap/loading observations and rejected captures remain disclosed, with no final performance or nonregression certification.

Related pull request: [#253](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/253).

### Reduced Temporary Allocation and Copying

Several authoritative owners now avoid repeated temporary work:

- Gameplay clock reads use the existing Session tick instead of constructing two full producer snapshots.
- Checkpoint-shared entities retain immutable installed definitions rather than deeply copying authored data on mutation.
- Mover-result transactions copy only the map state needed for their atomic rollback, not unrelated Session state for each record.
- Snapshot encoding writes directly into its publication storage and inserts Collision data in bulk; mutation journals and warm-reload captures retain explicit versions and Worker generations.
- BSP brush membership uses query-local paged bits instead of allocating membership trees. The traversal stack is allocated lazily, while encounter order, early-outs, malformed-input handling, and trace results remain checked.
- Warm map serialization avoids a duplicate 141,055,901-byte payload allocation. The recorded warm serialization comparison changes from 340.195 to 309 ms; no cold-loading or OS-resident-memory improvement is claimed.

The native same-command comparisons preserve complete tick/publication hashes and logical ownership. These are separate, staged comparisons with different replay scopes, not additive end-to-end savings. Some headed frame, input, loading, GPU, and RSS observations were unfavorable or inconclusive. Those records remain disclosed in the implementation PRs; lower allocation counts do not certify lower physical memory or uniformly faster gameplay.

Related pull requests: [#239](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/239), [#240](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/240), [#244](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/244), [#246](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/246), [#247](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/247), [#251](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/251), and [#252](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/252).

### Exact Lightmap Handoff

Eligible same-map replacement transfers the existing static lightmap owner instead of allocating and uploading an identical backing again. The native comparison eliminates one 34,521,088-byte allocation/upload and its measured 12.400 ms `writeTexture` call; lightmap peak API allocation bytes are halved in that case. Canonical GPU bytes and color/normal/depth output match, including ownership retirement and failure paths.

This is an exact eligible handoff, not a cross-map texture cache or a claim that physical RAM or steady frame rate improves by the same amount.

Related pull request: [#245](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/245).

## Loading, Packaging, and Delivery

The selected publication includes fifteen configured targets, with exact BSP/navigation/resource identities and complete immutable dependency graphs. Larger configured source objects such as Well and Freight have a bounded 128 MiB source-object publication allowance; ordinary object limits and full hash/length validation remain enforced.

The single configuration endpoint keeps its release-build cache-busting query. HTML and configuration remain `no-store`, while content-addressed scripts and assets retain immutable caching. Approved WASM and the complete generated JavaScript/helper closure remain checked together; page and Worker generation seals must agree with the selected package.

The uploader processes up to five objects concurrently, retaining full SHA/length readback and conditional creation. All leaf objects finish verification before resource roots publish, and roots finish before the catalog. One-second progress counts only verified bytes; failure stops new dispatch and drains in-flight work before closing the transport.

Related pull request: [#257](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/257).

## Additional Fixes

- Windows verification uses ordinary native Git checkouts, local builds, parameterized jobs, and ignored host configuration rather than intercepted production requests. Bounded preparation stages separate compiler and resource work without changing measured gameplay boundaries.
- Texture diagnostics distinguish live compressed/per-format allocation from cumulative creations/uploads, with creation, destruction, repeated-destruction, and replacement accounting.
- Non-GUI memory readers use bounded console-free, numeric-PID-scoped launches, while the game browser remains visibly headed. These tool fixes are not counted as game FPS improvements.
- Windows readback uses bounded plain text instead of recursively serializing provider-enriched strings from a small failed-launch log. Independent lifetime/private-memory limits protect the helper; the diagnosed stale helper was retired only after its exact identity and completed task were revalidated.
- Replay journals authenticate installed map/resource identity and requested Worker-generation transitions. Historical incompatible records remain explicit rather than being silently reinterpreted.

Related pull requests: [#235](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/235), [#243](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/243), [#246](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/246), [#247](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/247), [#253](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/253), and [#256](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/256).

## Current Scope

- Local single-player and bots only; no human networked multiplayer, Steam inventory/economy, or Source 2.
- Full physics-driven Upward Payload cart capture/victory and complete Demoman grenade/sticky rigid-body behavior remain unfinished. Successful setup-to-live combat is not a complete Payload match claim.
- Stable 60+ gameplay across Windows maps, bot counts, class transitions, and display sizes remains unverified. The retained 23-bot 2Fort case is below 60, and some cold-view or transition hitches remain.
- The release includes correctness-verified changes whose performance comparisons are unfavorable or inconclusive. It does not promise lower CPU, GPU, RAM, loading, or input latency for every workload.
- Large automatic same-team bot additions can exhaust the preset roster, and removing a fighting roster can leave an unresolved death-notice participant. Those reported lifecycle cases are not fixed in this cut.
- The formal exact-package predeployment cold/warm startup receipt was explicitly waived for v0.0.12 only. Asset hashes, generated binding compatibility, package integrity, and page/Worker generation checks remain mandatory. No formal startup certification is claimed.
