# playsrc v0.0.11

This update repairs the black-screen startup failure, adds playable Dustbowl and Gorge, and brings the local backpack and per-class loadout system to the browser. The supported inventory includes 22 non-stock weapons and a Burning Flames Team Captain, alongside the available stock equipment.

This is a focused release for `jump_beef`, `pl_upward`, `ctf_2fort`, `cp_dustbowl`, and `cp_gorge`. Additional CP/KOTH maps, further projectile weapons, environmental soundscape processing, and complete rigid-body gameplay remain separate work. Performance measurements below describe their actual captured scenarios, not a universal stable-60-FPS guarantee.

## Startup and Recovery

### Correct Production Generations

The v0.0.10 page and gameplay Worker embedded a compiled WASM generation different from the approved artifact named by the deployed configuration. Recovery failed before Worker and startup-movie initialization, and the error surface depended on VGUI resources that were not ready. The result could be a black screen without a useful visible explanation.

Production packaging now selects its configuration before bundling and binds the page, Worker, approved WASM, and complete generated JavaScript/helper closure together. Emitted generation seals are checked against the static configuration. Bootstrap failures display actionable text without depending on an already-initialized game renderer or VGUI.

When the browser blocks audible autoplay, a visible Play intro button lets the player start the movie. Upgrade recovery preserves the selected release's map scope and the loading descriptor field order expected by the previous application.

The release startup gate exercises the actual static package with startup movies enabled, including cold and warm movie-to-menu-to-playable-frame transitions. Asset delivery and a clean console alone are not startup acceptance.

Related pull request: [#231](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/231).

### Upward Particle Admission

Upward's configured cart/explosion particle closure exposed unsupported paths during world admission. The corrected implementation handles Z-aligned SpriteCard orientation, yaw spin, control-point point lighting, and child initialization from parent particles. It retains strict rejection of unsupported parameter modes instead of deleting particle roots or suppressing effects to make the map load.

Related pull request: [#232](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/232).

### Legacy Sky Admission

Configured legacy skies retain their base-texture records in HDR maps instead of failing world admission. Loading failures retain their diagnostic text rather than disappearing behind an incomplete loading surface.

Related pull request: [#231](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/231).

## World and Maps

### Dustbowl and Gorge

Dustbowl and Gorge are playable local Control Point maps with authenticated BSP, navigation, and resource dependencies. Attack/defend rules include setup and round timers, capture eligibility, overtime, scoring, team switching, restart behavior, entity I/O, and authored HUD presentation. Dustbowl also uses its multistage round progression and stage-dependent point and spawn state.

Headed match, restart, water, and 15/23-bot checks retain the stock simulation step. The recorded samples measured 66.59-66.77 ticks per second and 17.71-18.59 ms frame-interval p95. These are local verification samples, not Windows production performance certification.

Related pull request: [#216](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/216).

### Shared Control Point Rules

The shared local rules now also implement symmetric five-control-point rounds and King of the Hill. This includes walking bot capture paths, contested capture and decay, ownership prerequisites, respawn waves, team clocks, overtime, victory/restart, HUD, and announcers.

Those rule implementations do not make every declared map a release target. Badlands, Granary, Well, Freight, Gullywash, Process, Viaduct, Sawmill, Harvest, and Lakeside remain integration-only and are not included in this release's published map catalog.

Related pull requests: [#208](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/208) and [#209](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/209).

### Authored Smokestacks

Legacy `env_smokestack` effects now follow authored emission, velocity, wind, twist, roll, size, color, material selection, sorting, dormancy, and lifecycle rules on accepted client render frames. Stopping an emitter lets existing particles drain through their lifetimes; restart, occlusion, reentry, and map replacement retain their own state.

Verification includes all 46 emitters in the configured Lakeside fixture. That fixture is evidence for the effect implementation, not a claim that Lakeside has entered the release catalog.

Related pull request: [#226](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/226).

## Backpack and Loadouts

### One Local Copy of Each Supported Item

The Rust-owned catalog and equipment state provide a local inventory of 52 supported items: 29 stock items, 12 hitscan unlocks, 10 melee unlocks, and the Unusual Team Captain. Each implemented item has one local instance; no Steam inventory, trading, ownership service, or network account is required.

The authored backpack and per-class loadout UI apply class/slot eligibility, equipment changes, persistent selections, and standalone previews. Class-menu Edit Loadout uses that same owner. Equipment, remembered active/last-switched weapons, and death/restart behavior share the authoritative gameplay state rather than separate UI-only selections.

The final checks exercise equip, combat, persistence, death, and restart through the headed UI. Unfinished definitions remain excluded rather than appearing as working weapons.

Related pull request: [#206](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/206).

- Shotguns: Family Business, Reserve Shooter, and Panic Attack, with their authored class eligibility.
- Heavy: Natascha, Brass Beast, and Tomislav.
- Spy: Ambassador and Enforcer.

These use the shared equipment and weapon profiles, configured models, animations, sounds, attributes, and death-notice metadata. Local and bot actors retain the same gameplay authority.

### Ten Melee Unlocks

Southern Hospitality, Tribalman's Shiv, Powerjack, Bushwacka, Warrior's Spirit, Boston Basher, Back Scratcher, Fan O'War, Shahanshah, and Market Gardener are available through the same backpack/loadout system.

Headed checks include Basher self-damage, bleeding across holster/respawn, moving and occluded Fan O'War markers, and equipment persistence after application reload.

Two final five-second local samples retained 335/334 simulation ticks, 17.080/17.060 ms frame-interval p95, and no intervals over 20 ms. Windows certification is not inferred from those Mac measurements.

## Performance and Responsiveness

### Render-Critical Worker Replies

Chromium could defer already-posted gameplay Worker replies after discrete input, even when visibility computation itself took approximately 0.2 ms. Bounded, ordered atomic completion now delivers those replies with Rust leases, generation checks, cancellation, and explicit payload ownership instead of waiting on that posted-message scheduling path.

In the final matched full-roster Mac capture, all 1,447 gameplay replies use atomic delivery. Visibility RPC maximum falls from 17.790 to 4.610 ms, and input-to-visible maximum falls from 22.195 to 7.565 ms, with 66.693 Hz simulation and no gaps over 50 ms or empty full rolling buckets. This does not change input sampling, simulation cadence, graphics quality, or browser security settings.

Related pull requests: [#203](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/203) and [#220](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/220).

### Less Repeated Entity and Model Work

Entity checkpoints share unchanged indexes through copy-on-write while preserving transactional mutation and rollback. The controlled 1,024-entity checkpoint reduces allocations from 2,211 to 2.

Animated presentation models retain immutable bone-palette membership instead of rescanning selected primitive vertices during steady pose encoding. Configured parity covers 295 models and 3,248 selections. Corrected Worker analysis records 45 geometry-scan samples before and zero after; mean model transaction time changes from 0.462 to 0.260 ms in the captured scenario, with world/viewmodel color, normal, and depth parity retained.

These changes do not imply uniformly lower memory or GPU timings. Unfavorable compositor, input, loading, and aggregate-memory observations remain in their individual comparisons; allocation estimates and process sums are not unique retained-memory measurements.

Related pull requests: [#215](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/215) and [#218](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/218).

### Reachable Pipeline Reuse

Particle and authored door/resupply programs prepare within bounded generation ownership while preserving on-demand texture residency. Shared static-prop fade materials keep occurrence-specific values independent and reuse the existing graph owner.

Cold/warm, replacement, resize, and door-motion checks show zero corresponding first-use particle/prop pipelines. The separate static-prop replacement comparison removes the recorded 3.455 ms node build with unchanged peak texture residency and matching pixels/depth. Player-material preparation also survives normal map replacement.

Related pull requests: [#205](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/205), [#212](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/212), and [#224](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/224).

## Loading, Packaging, and Delivery

The published catalog contains the original three targets plus Dustbowl and Gorge, all pinned to configured TF2 content build `24245096`. Every selected map retains its exact BSP, navigation/resources, dependency ledger, and referenced immutable chunks. Integration-only declarations do not enter the production catalog automatically.

Production package verification keeps the approved WASM and its generated binding closure coherent with the page, Worker, and configuration. The startup gate checks visible behavior, not merely successful HTTP responses or matching artifact names.

## Additional Fixes

- CPU-profile replay reconstructs signed timestamps for out-of-order samples instead of inflating totals by clamping negative deltas. Sampling estimates and unattributed tails remain explicit.
- Immutable manifests bind main/Worker CPU and allocation evidence to their owners, capture plans, and clock boundaries. Retained-memory gauges are reported as before/after values, not misleading cumulative-counter deltas.
- Opt-in render-owner probes retain identities and dependency evidence without claiming unproven redundant work or an FPS gain.
- macOS capture admission identifies the measured page's actual native window and rejects ambiguous windows, same-browser popups, or covering system layers instead of relying on page focus alone.
- Profile preflight honors measured minimum remaining-runtime requirements and retires only authenticated owned development services after failure, preserving FIFO handoff and the existing time cap.
- Test-only allocation measurement ownership fixes the native parallel-test race without changing production accounting.
- Generated screenshots and videos are transient PR attachments, not repository files. Tracked-media checks and batch-import guards prevent their reintroduction.

## Current Scope

- Gameplay remains local single-player and bots; human networked multiplayer and Source 2 are not included.
- Release maps are `jump_beef`, `pl_upward`, `ctf_2fort`, `cp_dustbowl`, and `cp_gorge`. The ten additional integration-only CP/KOTH targets are not advertised as released maps.
- The inventory includes supported stock equipment, 12 hitscan unlocks, 10 melee unlocks, and one Burning Flames Team Captain. Unsupported definitions remain unavailable.
- Grenade Launcher, Stickybomb Launcher, Sapper, and Original are not offered in the backpack. Saved unavailable grenade/sticky selections normalize to supported equipment.
- Complete Stickybomb Launcher/pipe-grenade rigid-body behavior and the full physics-driven Upward Payload cart remain unfinished. The separate projectile expansion is not part of this release.
- Complete environmental soundscape/DSP playback remains unfinished; soundscape work is not presented as finished ambience.
- Stable 60+ completed gameplay FPS without stalls across every Windows workload remains a verification target, not a blanket release claim. Browser VSync remains capability-gated.
