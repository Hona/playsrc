# playsrc v0.1.0

Demoman's Stickybomb Launcher is now playable: charge a shot, stick bombs to the world, detonate them, and blast jump with the resulting impulse. Launcher sounds, impacts, and the charge and sticky-count HUD accompany the action.

This update also corrects menu borders, keyboard ownership, and startup failure handling. The release retains all 15 maps: `jump_beef`, `pl_upward`, `ctf_2fort`, `cp_dustbowl`, `cp_gorge`, `cp_badlands`, `cp_granary`, `cp_well`, `cp_freight_final1`, `cp_gullywash_final1`, `cp_process_final`, `koth_viaduct`, `koth_sawmill`, `koth_harvest_final`, and `koth_lakeside_final`.

## Gameplay and HUD

### Stickybomb Launcher

- Sticky and grenade physics follow their session-owned collision and cleanup lifecycle, including charging, sticking, detonation, and blast impulses.
- Hitscan, melee, explosions, and airblast interact with projectiles in order. Bone-follower colliders remain independent.
- Demoman's HUD displays charge and deployed pipe count; launcher, impact, and deflection sounds follow gameplay events.

The supported local inventory grows from 62 to 64 unique implemented items.

The accepted headed gameplay check covers charging, firing, visible sticking, detonation, blast jumping, and HUD updates. Its ten-second audio capture has no mismatches, uncovered samples, or underruns.

Related pull request: [#143](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/143).

### Keyboard and Menu Controls

Held gameplay keys retain ownership through browser key repeats. Physical crouch chords work without taking over unrelated browser shortcuts. Menu buttons use their authored state borders, viewport-scaled image corners, and case-insensitive corner masks instead of generic rounded backgrounds.

Related pull requests: [#263](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/263), [#264](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/264), and [#267](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/267).

## Loading, Packaging, and Delivery

Fatal startup errors stay visible even when movie callbacks race with failure handling. Media and threaded-WASM failures retain their specific startup stage. The application graph is preloaded from HTML, and package identity authenticates both bootstrap and split main-script generations.

Renderer retirement now releases conversion, canvas-depth, and multisample attachments before discarding their ownership records. Collision traversal reuses storage during sustained bot movement; no end-to-end speedup is claimed.

Related pull requests: [#266](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/266) and [#261](https://github.com/Hona/playsrc/pull/261).

## Current Scope

Play remains local, with bots; human multiplayer and networking are not included. The standalone native/WASM map-byte comparison still reports the previously reproduced sentry default-pose geometry discrepancy. This update does not claim to fix that renderer limitation or certify the reported iPhone loading/black-screen issue as resolved.
