# Physics Roadmap

[`../../../docs/roadmap-contract.md`](../../../docs/roadmap-contract.md) defines the normative roadmap schema and denominator review gate. [`../../../TERMINOLOGY.md`](../../../TERMINOLOGY.md) defines delivery statuses, coverage classifications, inventory, evidence, and Complete.

## Completion Denominator

This leaf roadmap contains exactly 41 behavior rows. [`inventories/body-and-constraint-families.md`](inventories/body-and-constraint-families.md) contains 30 candidate body-motion, body-shape, body-role, relationship, constraint, controller, and articulated-assembly items. Those candidates contribute 0 denominator items until a checked-in generator emits the inventory and a denominator review accepts this roadmap. The current completion denominator is therefore 41 behavior rows. Acceptance of all 30 candidate inventory items without a roadmap change would make the denominator 71 items.

The denominator covers isolated physics environments; bounded body and relationship lifecycle; static, kinematic, motion-disabled, and dynamic rigid-body state; transforms; velocity; mass; inertia; gravity; damping; aerodynamic drag; force, impulse, and torque application; activation and sleeping; body-pair selection; broad-phase synchronization; continuous collision; persistent contacts and manifolds; physical surface response; penetration recovery; stacking; the accepted constraint and controller families; moving geometry; fluids; bone followers; ragdoll state; breakable state; physical callbacks; fixed-tick advancement; deterministic ordering; numerical and resource bounds; single-writer concurrency; continuation snapshots; and gameplay/entity seams.

The denominator is Not accepted. The generated inventory command and exact target-game occurrence registries are Missing; persistent-contact ownership is unresolved at the collision boundary; the physical surface-property producer is not declared by its adjacent owner; BSP, PHY, Collision, and Physics disagree about collision-payload and shape production; and numeric performance budgets and platform profiles have not been selected.

## Inputs

- One environment definition containing a stable environment identity, positive fixed tick interval, Source-space gravity vector, non-negative air density, arithmetic profile, surface-registry identity, collision-world identity, and performance profile. The TF2 server comparison profile uses its declared simulation tick and the public server setting of 10 maximum collisions per object per timestep; no caller can substitute render-frame duration.
- Immutable canonical collision shapes and stateless geometric query results supplied by `packages/world/collision`. Each shape reference has one stable identity, source association, local transform, bounds, contents, and physical-surface identity. Physics never parses BSP, PHY, StudioModel, or collision-shape bytes.
- Ordered body definitions. Each definition supplies a stable body identity; one accepted motion class and shape family from [`inventories/body-and-constraint-families.md`](inventories/body-and-constraint-families.md); transform; linear and local-axis angular velocity; mass; local inertia; local center of mass; volume; gravity, drag, motion, collision, and trigger state; physical-surface identity; contents and generic pair-filter keys; and an opaque owner identity used only in emitted events.
- Ordered relationship definitions for pair exclusions, indexed collision sets, springs, constraints, constraint groups, controllers, fluids, bone followers, ragdolls, and breakable fragment sets. Every body reference names a body in the same environment or the operation is `Missing`.
- One ordered command batch per physics tick. Commands can create, destroy, or transfer bodies and relationships; set transforms, velocities, mass properties, materials, flags, and controller targets; apply center or offset impulses and torque impulses; wake or sleep bodies; change pair policy; activate or deactivate constraints; and request a supplied fragment transition. Every command carries tick, producer sequence, operation identity, and stable target identity.
- Pure pair-filter, penetration-policy, and pre-solve-response inputs supplied by Simulation and the selected game. Physics invokes them with immutable body/contact views in declared order; they return bounded typed dispositions and cannot mutate a physics world.
- Resolved physical surface records containing exact identity, friction, elasticity, density, thickness, and dampening. The adjacent public owner for parsing and resolving these records is unresolved. Physics applies the records and never owns audio, particle, decal, or game-material effects.
- Ordered kinematic transforms, shadow targets, bone transforms, vehicle controls, and ragdoll activation inputs supplied by their owning Entity, Movement, StudioModel, or game integration. Physics does not derive animation, mover trajectories, player commands, or game intent.
- A required limits record containing `maxEnvironments`, `maxBodies`, `maxStaticBodies`, `maxKinematicBodies`, `maxDynamicBodies`, `maxBodyShapeReferences`, `maxPairExclusions`, `maxCollisionSetElements`, `maxConstraints`, `maxConstraintGroups`, `maxSprings`, `maxControllers`, `maxFluids`, `maxBoneFollowers`, `maxRagdollBodies`, `maxFragmentsPerBreak`, `maxCommandsPerTick`, `maxCandidatePairs`, `maxNarrowPhasePairs`, `maxPersistentContacts`, `maxManifolds`, `maxPointsPerManifold`, `maxSolverIslands`, `maxBodiesPerIsland`, `maxSolverIterations`, `maxContinuousIterations`, `maxPenetrationIterations`, `maxSubsteps`, `maxEventsPerTick`, `maxSnapshotBytes`, `maxScratchBytes`, and `maxConcurrentReaders`. Every field is a non-negative integer and no operation has an unbounded mode.
- Valve Source SDK 2013 commit `88fa198fba3fb85d46d4c95018254693fdc3af0a`: `src/public/vphysics_interface.h`; `src/public/vphysics/{constraints.h,friction.h,performance.h,player_controller.h,vehicles.h,collision_set.h,object_hash.h}`; `src/game/shared/{baseentity_shared.cpp,physics_shared.h,physics_shared.cpp,ragdoll_shared.h,ragdoll_shared.cpp,props_shared.h}`; and `src/game/server/{physics.h,physics.cpp,physics_collisionevent.h,physconstraint.h,physconstraint.cpp,physics_bone_follower.h,physics_bone_follower.cpp,props.h,props.cpp,physics_impact_damage.h,physics_impact_damage.cpp}`. TF2 integration also uses the public TF2 grenade, prop, and collision-group consumers at that commit.
- Controlled target observations for fixed body, contact, constraint, fluid, controller, ragdoll, bone-follower, break, event, and tick scenarios. Each capture supplies identical initial state, command sequence, tick interval, physical surfaces, and collision geometry.

## Outputs

- One `PhysicsWorld` per environment containing monotonic world revision, simulation tick and time, stable body and relationship tables, broad-phase synchronization revision, persistent contact/manifold state, solver-island state, controller state, fluid membership, sleeping state, and next event sequence.
- One ordered `PhysicsTransitionBatch` per completed tick containing created, changed, transferred, sleeping, waking, and destroyed identities; constraint state changes; contact transitions; body transforms and velocities; and the resulting world revision. A failed tick publishes no partial transition batch.
- A body-state record containing motion class, shape identity, transform, linear velocity, local-axis angular velocity, mass and inverse mass, local inertia and inverse inertia, center of mass, volume, energy, material identity, enabled flags, sleep state, and current environment identity.
- A persistent-contact snapshot containing stable body pair, manifold identity, ordered points, oriented normal, penetration depth, normal force, absorbed energy, friction coefficient, surface identities, lifetime state, and valid tick. Stateless geometric contacts remain Collision output and never become solver state without Physics admission.
- Ordered physical events for matched pre/post collision observations, contact start/end, friction, fluid enter/leave, trigger enter/leave, wake/sleep, constraint break, body transfer, and fragment transition. Collision events include pre/post linear and angular velocities, contact point and normal, relative contact speed, collision speed, surfaces, and solved impulse data required by game-owned damage policy.
- An immutable `PhysicsSnapshot` sufficient to restore every Physics-owned body, relationship, contact, controller, fluid, sleep, ordering, and fixed-tick state bit and reproduce the next transition against the same collision world, surface registry, arithmetic profile, and limits. Physics defines no file, wire, or compatibility encoding for this value.
- A structured error naming environment, tick, producer sequence, operation, stable target identities, offending scalar or count, configured limit, violated invariant, and exactly one `Malformed`, `Unsupported`, `Unknown`, or `Missing` classification.

## Invariants

- Public position, distance, velocity, force impulse, and torque impulse units follow `vphysics_interface.h`: Source XYZ inches, seconds, kilograms, degrees, kilograms-inches-per-second, and kilograms-degrees-per-second. Angular velocity is stored relative to body-local axes; world-space contact velocity is derived from transform, center velocity, and local angular velocity.
- Initial body mass is in `[0.1, 50000]` kilograms. A runtime mass mutation accepts only finite positive mass and caps the stored result at `50000`; it never silently creates a static body. Static state, motion-disabled state, and zero inverse mass are distinct observable states.
- Static bodies never integrate. Kinematic bodies follow supplied transforms and expose derived surface velocity without accepting force-driven displacement. Dynamic bodies integrate while motion is enabled. Disabling dynamic motion retains dynamic identity, transform, velocity, mass, contacts, and constraints and does not reclassify the body as static.
- One stable identity names each environment, body, shape reference, constraint, spring, controller, fluid, contact, manifold, and event for its complete lifetime. Allocation addresses, container slots, broad-phase nodes, worker schedules, and contact-cache slots never become identity or tie-break inputs.
- A body command batch is validated in full before mutation. Commands then apply by producer sequence. A command targeting an identity destroyed by an earlier command is `Missing`; duplicate creation is `Malformed`; a failed command prevents tick publication and leaves the prior authoritative world unchanged.
- A tick synchronizes every accepted transform exactly once, produces stable body-pair candidates, admits or retires contacts, solves connected islands, integrates dynamic state, updates sleeping state, and emits one final immutable snapshot. Render frames never advance or partially observe these phases.
- Broad phase may return false positives and cannot omit a pair whose exhaustive accepted-shape test produces a required contact or continuous hit. Narrow-phase geometry remains Collision-owned; Physics owns candidate consumption, persistent manifold lifetime, response, and solver ordering.
- Pair filtering applies environment equality, enabled collision state, body role, explicit pair exclusion, indexed collision-set result, contents intersection, generic pair keys, game-owned pure predicate, and pre-solve disposition in that order. Rejection retires existing contacts deterministically before event publication.
- Persistent contacts are keyed by stable body pair and stable geometric features. Point replacement, contact start/end, normal orientation, warm state, and friction state are deterministic under the declared arithmetic profile. Pure geometric overlap alone is not a persistent contact event.
- Physical pair response uses `clamp(surfaceA.friction × surfaceB.friction, 0, 1)` and `clamp(surfaceA.elasticity × surfaceB.elasticity, 0, 1)`. Density, thickness, and dampening retain their supplied units and affect only their declared rigid-body, fluid, or contact calculation. Unknown or Missing surface identity cannot become a default surface inside Physics.
- Force and torque inputs are impulses unless the selected motion-controller result explicitly declares acceleration or force integrated over the current fixed tick. Center impulses change linear velocity; offset impulses also change local angular velocity about the current center of mass; static, kinematic, and motion-disabled bodies ignore force-driven displacement without consuming a different body's impulse.
- Sleeping removes a dynamic body from integration but not from identity, pair policy, contacts, constraints, fluids, or snapshots. Explicit wake, accepted nonzero velocity or impulse, enabled motion, active controller target, moving contact, constraint activation, or fluid wake request follows the accepted wake matrix. Wake/sleep events are edge-triggered once per state change.
- Continuous collision uses supplied relative-motion casts for accepted fast body/world and body/body pairs. Look-ahead, substep, collision-count, and iteration limits come from the selected performance profile; reaching a bound returns a classified result and never tunnels by switching to a discrete-only fallback.
- Penetration recovery is a bounded solver operation selected by one pure penetration disposition. Recovery cannot teleport an unrelated body, change game collision policy, create damage, or suppress a retained penetration diagnostic. Exhausting the accepted iteration or correction bound leaves the prior published tick authoritative.
- Constraint endpoints belong to one environment. Destroying or transferring an endpoint deterministically deactivates or transfers every affected relationship before the body changes environment. Constraint break thresholds of zero mean unbreakable; one threshold crossing produces one inactive state and one break event.
- Ragdoll physical state contains at most 24 body elements. Its body order, parent references, collision-set choices, three-axis joint state, animated-friction input, sleep result, and solved transforms remain Physics state; skeleton pose production, death policy, retention policy, and pixels remain external.
- A fragment transition is atomic: retire the source body's contacts and relationships, remove it, create the supplied ordered fragment bodies, apply supplied inherited and burst impulses, and emit one transition. Physics never chooses whether an entity breaks, which fragments exist, damage amount, health, model, effect, or sound.
- Event ordering is `(tick, substep, event-kind rank, lower body identity, higher body identity, relationship identity, contact identity, event sequence)`. Matched pre/post collision observations share one contact-attempt identity. Consumer commands caused by emitted events enter the next declared Physics command phase; reentrant world mutation is prohibited.
- Mutable world advancement is single-writer. Concurrent calls that create, mutate, transfer, or step the same environment fail with `InvalidPhase`. Immutable completed snapshots support up to `maxConcurrentReaders` readers and produce bit-identical ordered reads without serializing those readers through the mutable world.
- Every scalar is finite before use. Every count, product, sum, transform, inverse, allocation, candidate visit, narrow-phase request, contact point, island, solver iteration, event, snapshot byte, and scratch byte is checked against fixed numeric rules and the required limits before mutation or allocation.
- Physics snapshots contain only current-contract structured state. Simulation, Networking, Replay, tools, and applications may encode or transport them but cannot redefine Physics state, retain a compatibility schema, or restore a snapshot against different collision, surface, arithmetic, or limits identities.

## Ownership Exclusions

- BSP headers, lumps, model-block framing, and exact bytes belong to `packages/formats/bsp`. PHY headers, collision payloads, convex records, physical keydata syntax, and decoded runtime-neutral records belong to `packages/formats/phy`.
- Immutable collision-shape construction, contents and masks, support queries, ray and hull casts, overlaps, stateless geometric contacts, broad-phase query structures, and narrow phase belong to `packages/world/collision`. Physics owns only dynamic body state, persistent contacts/manifolds, physical response, and solver integration over those results.
- Physical surface-record parsing, inheritance, name resolution, and registry provenance require an adjacent public owner. Physics owns application of supplied friction, elasticity, density, thickness, and dampening. Audio, particle, decal, movement, and game meanings of the same surface remain with their owners.
- Entity identity, classname behavior, lifecycle, hierarchy, I/O, trigger lifecycle, mover request/completion, break decision, fragment selection, and forwarding an event to an owning entity belong to `packages/world/entity` and the selected game.
- Player command movement, hull selection, stairs, ground state, water level, base velocity, mover trajectory, push rollback, blocker choice, and carried-player displacement belong to `packages/runtime/movement` and the selected game. Physics applies only supplied controller targets and physical responses.
- Simulation owns the gameplay tick and cross-module phase schedule. Physics owns deterministic ordering only inside one Simulation-invoked Physics phase and cannot advance gameplay authority independently.
- TF2, CS:S, and legacy Source 1 CS:GO own collision-group extensions and pair decisions, damage and health policy, prop and projectile behavior, ragdoll creation/retention policy, vehicle control meaning, and game-specific callback responses. Physics invokes typed pure inputs and emits physical facts.
- StudioModel and the selected animation owner produce skeleton and bone transforms. Physics advances ragdoll and bone-follower bodies but does not parse models, evaluate animation, or produce a render pose.
- Rendering owns ragdoll, prop, fragment, and bone-follower presentation. Audio and Particle own impact, scrape, break, strain, splash, and effect selection. Physics emits no pixel, GPU, audio, particle, or decal work.
- Networking and Replay own wire and recorded-state interpretation. Asset Store owns durable artifacts. Tools own inventory generation, captures, benchmarks, and inspection. Applications cannot embed another rigid-body solver.
- Persistent save-game file formats are excluded from the current Active targets. A future accepted owner requirement must add any such serialization behavior to this denominator before implementation.
